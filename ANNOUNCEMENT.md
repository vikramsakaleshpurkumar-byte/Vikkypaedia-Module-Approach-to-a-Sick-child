# Announcement — Approach to the Sick Child (v3, 2026)

## Substack post

A child who dies in hospital usually dies on the first day, and usually after a period in which someone could have noticed. The blood pressure was normal. The heart rate was not. The chart said so, and nobody was called.

This year the paediatric sepsis guidelines changed. For the first time in years, one of the recommendations is strong, and it is a recommendation *against* something. In a hospital without intensive care, do not give a fluid bolus to a septic child who is not hypotensive. That sentence comes from FEAST, a trial many of us taught around rather than from.

I have rebuilt *Approach to the Sick Child* around that kind of honesty. It has 20 units and covers triage at the door; shock and when fluid kills; seizures, poisons, snakes and scorpions; the malnourished child in whom every rule inverts; and the young infant in whom serious illness looks like almost nothing. Every unit sets the ideal setting and the district hospital side by side, and every unit has an India lens.

It is free and works offline on a phone. It measures what you remember, not what you opened.
